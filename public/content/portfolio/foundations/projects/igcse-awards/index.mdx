# Introduction

In 2023, I achieved outstanding results in my **IGCSE examinations** at Marlborough College Malaysia, earning **10A\* and 2A** grades. I received multiple "Best in Region" awards:

- **Best in Asia** for Computer Science
- **Best in Malaysia** for Design Technology
- **Best in Malaysia** for Physics
- **Best in Malaysia** for Chemistry
