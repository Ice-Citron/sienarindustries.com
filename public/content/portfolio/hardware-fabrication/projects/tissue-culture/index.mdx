# Introduction

During Summer 2023, I interned at **Nosco Biotechnology** in Singapore as a graphics designer. While there, the lab technician introduced me to tissue culture — and I got hooked. I ended up conducting tissue culture of black ginger (a cash crop and traditional medicine) at home, building all the equipment I needed from scratch.

This project combined biotechnology, DIY engineering, and design. I built a DIY autoclave from a pressure cooker, constructed a sterile transfer box with a soldering iron, and even set up a mist irrigation system for growing the plants.

Since I didn't have access to professional lab equipment at home, I improvised:

- **DIY Autoclave** — Converted a standard pressure cooker into an autoclave capable of reaching 124°C+ through increased pressure, sufficient for sterilising growth media and tools
- **Sterile Transfer Box** — Built using a soldering iron to cut and shape plastic, kept sterilised with 30% Chlorox solution between transfers
- **Culture Media** — Prepared Murashige and Skoog (MS) media with agar, vitamins, and growth hormones
